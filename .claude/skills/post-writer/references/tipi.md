# Structure per content type

## mercato — report commentary
1. Hook: the most surprising figure, with its unit and year.
2. Context in 1–2 lines (who published it, on what).
3. What it means for developers/SGR selling new builds (our angle).
4. One concrete implication or question for the reader.
Visual: `data` card (one figure) or carousel (3–5 figures). Source in the first comment and on the card.

## piattaforma — platform marketing
1. Hook: a real situation of a developer or buyer (problem, not product).
2. How it works today, in plain steps.
3. What changes with gCASTEL (only existing features).
4. Soft call to action (demo, message).
Visual: carousel "come funziona" or `announce`.

## filiera — industry theme / thought leadership
1. Hook: a clear stance.
2. Why now (regulation, data, trend) with a source.
3. 2–3 arguments.
4. Open question.
Talk about BIM, data, AI as **industry themes**, never as our features.

## evento — events (three moments)
- Before (7–10 days): what, where, when, who from gCASTEL, why come and talk to us.
- Live: photo from the team + one insight heard.
- After (within 2 days): 3 learnings, thanks (tag only with OK).
Visual: `announce` (before), team photo (live/after).

## feature — release (only when Ettore sends [FEATURE])
1. Hook: the problem it solves for whom.
2. What it does, in one sentence.
3. How it works (2–3 steps) — screenshot if provided.
4. Who can use it from when. Respect the embargo.

## partnership — announcement (only with [PARTNERSHIP])
1. Who the partner is and why together.
2. What changes for clients.
3. Quote from both sides if provided (never invented).
Tag the partner only with OK. Partner must have approved the text.

## vita — team, programs, milestones
Short, human, concrete. One photo or `quote` card.

## gilberto — founder's profile
First person. A scene from the field (a meeting, a negotiation, a question from a developer), the lesson, a stance. Personal background only as Gilberto or Ettore provide it. No corporate tone; no product pitch in more than one post out of four.
